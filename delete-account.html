Delete your Bandrise account and data

In the app

  1. Open Bandrise.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data, then Delete.

This deletes straight away: your account, credits, test attempts, saved words, AI feedback and credit history. Downloaded tests are removed from your phone. The only thing left for a short time is a counter of your AI requests (account ID and a number), which is removed after about two days of AI use in the app.

Without the app

Email awais9876khan@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bandrise data". The app has no sign-in, so please send your Account ID: in the app, open Profile and tap Contact us, and it is added to the email for you. We delete everything within 30 days and reply when it is done.

If you have already removed the app, we may not be able to find your data, because it is not linked to your name or email. Please write to us with the date you used the app and we will help if we can.
